## Withheld activation boundaries

- Legacy shielded scheme `0x01` is disabled fail-closed. Public history remains
  readable, but private proof generation, verification, submission, CLI, and
  wallet actions stay disabled. No privacy claim is authorized until a new
  ownership-binding scheme is independently reviewed and versioned.
- Existing-network Staking V2 activation requires an exact live pre-state,
  owner confirmation, a common future epoch boundary, deterministic dry-run on
  all four preserved databases, and at least one complete post-activation epoch.
  Shipping the binary does not activate the marker.
- No Accounting V2/V3 contract migration is implicit in the binary release.
  Each migration requires its own fixed source snapshot, governed pause/seal/
  completion, source-bound manifest, custody proof, receipts, and post-state
  parity.
- Archive V2 roles, legacy retirement, storage reclamation, and R2 deletion are
  not activated by this qualification. Exact role/capacity gates and separate
  deletion authorization remain mandatory.

## Remaining signed-testnet gates

1. Review the final diff and secret scan, remove local build/test artifacts,
   commit and push the candidate, and pass every hosted branch gate.
2. Merge through the protected branch, create the exact annotated v0.5.264 tag,
   and pass the release workflow, including its clean four-validator run.
3. Download only workflow-produced artifacts, verify checksums and GitHub
   attestations, attach the detached post-quantum checksum signature, and keep
   the release draft until live acceptance.
4. Perform bounded read-only fleet preflight. Fail closed on artifact, hash,
   signer, key, environment, WAL, archive, health, disk, or common-tip mismatch.
5. Stage the signed artifact and use one coordinated four-host
   stop/install/start. Preserve v0.5.263 rollback material and prohibit a
   mixed-version rolling restart.
6. Prove installed/running artifact parity, key/signer/environment/WAL
   preservation, guarded BFT readiness, common advancing slots and hashes, all
   four current voters, public/RPC/WebSocket acceptance, outage/rejoin, and
   genesis-to-tip archive parity before publishing the release.

## Mainnet blockers

Mainnet remains blocked until the live candidate has sustained SLO evidence,
the archive plane has durable capacity and independent failure domains for
genesis-to-tip retention, signed artifact and rollback operations are rehearsed,
and every production dependency/admin surface is fail-closed. Privacy cannot be
advertised while only the disabled legacy scheme exists. Any existing-state
Staking V2 or contract migration requires separately accepted governance and
replay evidence. The current 200 GB validator storage profile is not approved
for mainnet or indefinite archive growth.
