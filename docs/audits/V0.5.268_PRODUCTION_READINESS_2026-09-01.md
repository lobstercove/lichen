## Failure lineage and corrective scope

Immutable `v0.5.267` passed all protected-main CI jobs and its tag's release
quality, security, compiler-sandbox, and genesis-bundle gates. Its first Archive
V2 attempt timed out while short RPC probes were starved after an intentional
corrupt-segment restart even though the validators continued advancing. One
evidence-based failed-job retry passed that point and the complete Archive V2
matrix, including corruption quarantine, replica repair, source outage,
catalog-bound checkpointing, and fresh full/cache/consensus role joins.

That retry then failed the strict volume journey: both margin `open_position`
calls returned ABI code 6, meaning the mark price was missing or older than the
contract's 750-slot freshness bound. The long Archive V2/restart matrix had
correctly aged the genesis-seeded price, while the journey prepared collateral,
allowances, and insurance but did not refresh its controlled margin price. The
position lookup and close failures were downstream consequences. The tag
published no artifacts and is not rewritten or rerun blindly.

The first local `v0.5.268` qualification iteration reached the strict volume
journey after the complete Archive V2 matrix, then rejected its attempted
direct `set_mark_price` transaction because the margin administrator is the
governed governance authority. The normal local validator price feeder kept the
mark fresh enough for both opens and the close to pass, but the explicit
refresh assertion correctly failed the gate (140 passed, 1 failed). No commit,
PR, tag, artifact, or fleet mutation resulted from that iteration. The
correction below uses the canonical native consensus-oracle path instead of the
governed contract-call override, which the deterministic post-block mirror
would overwrite in any event.

`v0.5.268` preserves the reviewed runtime and its fail-closed stale-price rule.
The strict volume journey now resolves every active local validator keypair and
submits a controlled LICN/USD native oracle attestation from each. The canonical
stake quorum advances the consensus source slot, and the deterministic
post-block mirror refreshes pair 1 immediately before positions open. This
avoids both a noncanonical direct storage override and dependence on external
price-feed availability. A tracked release-gate asset assertion requires that
refresh to remain ordered before the first margin open.
