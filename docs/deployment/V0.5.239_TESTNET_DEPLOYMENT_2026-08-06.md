`84ec5fb4907f70d3c5916eb226955fda5e7acd35` is installed and running on all

source-restored genesis row while Archive V2 is disabled.

## Initial deployment and live acceptance

All four validators were stopped before binary promotion and started from the
same signed artifact. EU then replayed its preserved gap using ordinary
source-backed 100-block range responses and caught up without copied state or
a checkpoint shortcut.

An interrupted Archive V2 retirement pass later held EU's RocksDB lock during
its initial equivalence scan. It was stopped while the retirement journal was
still absent, proving the command had not reached deletion. EU restarted from
its unchanged state. The live three-validator quorum advanced too quickly for
v0.5.239's zero-slot pre-consensus admission gate, leaving EU caught up and
broadcasting votes but not yet inside its BFT proposal loop. SEA and IN were
briefly paused to freeze the tip. EU reached exact parity and logged entry into
BFT at height `10,235,558`; SEA and IN then both re-entered BFT from that same
height before finality resumed.

Final acceptance evidence:

  the usual one-slot cross-origin skew);

  identity: EU 10, SEA 6, IN 12, and US 13;
- EU appeared in all 41 sampled canonical commit certificates;

## Online-safe cleanup

The signed v0.5.239 staging directory was removed from `/tmp` on every host
after installed/running hash verification. Six obsolete v0.5.238 restage
trees were also removed from US. Package archives were cleaned and archived
system journals were bounded to 64 MiB. The validated staging paths accounted
for about 755 MB apparent bytes on US and 145 MB on each other host; sparse or
shared extents and concurrent chain growth make the net filesystem gain
smaller than those apparent sizes.

No live binary, rollback binary, validator database, cold archive, WAL, key,
identity, V2 catalog/segment/retirement proof, evidence directory, source
devchain, or R2 object was removed.
