## Deployment

The four validators were coordinated-stopped before installation. Official
tag-workflow artifacts were staged, hash-verified, atomically promoted, and
started together. No locally built binary was installed. Every host preserves
the v0.5.229 and v0.5.228 signed rollback executables; validator state, cold
history, consensus WAL, keys, identities, access configuration, and provider
backup storage were preserved.

The repository release verifier completed successfully on all four hosts.
Final acceptance reported `lichen-validator 0.5.236`, the exact installed and
`/proc/<pid>/exe` hash above, `status=ok`, and `NRestarts=0` everywhere.

The final operating topology is:

- US: validator, faucet, and custody active; local health endpoints return 200.
- EU: validator active; faucet and custody intentionally disabled. Legacy cold
  maintenance has a one-hour startup delay and 300-second interval so the node
  can remain in consensus after its capacity-bound catch-up.
- SEA: validator and faucet active; custody intentionally disabled.
- IN: validator and faucet active; custody intentionally disabled.

EU initially remained in one-block catch-up behind a three-validator quorum.
US and SEA were briefly stopped, leaving IN as the sync source without quorum;
EU and IN then matched the same tip hash. US and SEA were restarted together.
EU subsequently reported fresh activity/voting at slot `10,172,351`, within one
slot of the other validators.

## Public-history manifest evidence

The stopped fixed-tip full public-history scan was started over all 21
categories at slot `10,170,477`. EU and IN completed independently and matched
exactly:

- manifest root: `271949078ded8fdc9eb6bcefd3fa7279c418bbd8ecf64aa8b34c98002717d397`
- canonical blocks: 8,743,484
- transactions: 5,814,836

This is valid two-way genesis-to-tip evidence, not a completed four-way gate.
A separate concurrent operator/session started validators while US and SEA
scanners were still running. SEA then failed while iterating `account_txs`
because primary RocksDB compaction removed SST `154067.sst` beneath the
read-only scanner; US was also invalidated and aborted. The incident produced
no manifest mismatch, but the four-way full-manifest hard gate remains open and
must be repeated with exclusive fleet ownership and secondary/checkpoint-safe
scanner semantics. Do not represent this run as four-way manifest parity.
